READ WITH CONFIDENCE

What these results mean.

Authenticated, not endorsed

A matching creator means the expected wallet published the record. It doesn't prove a real-world organization owns that wallet.

Evidence can be missing

Without recent independent checks, availability is unknown. That doesn't mean the website is definitely down or safe.

Stay in control

A pinned wallet is remembered only in this browser. Resetting trust removes local rollback history and should be treated cautiously.
