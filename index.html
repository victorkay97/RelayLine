MADE FOR CONTINUITY

A second route. A clearer signal.

RelayLine separates publishing from discovery so people can still find an authorized route when the original service is unavailable.

01 / PUBLISH

Create a recovery record

In your wallet-connected workspace, register a primary website and an alternate HTTPS address. Publishing happens only after you approve a testnet transaction.

02 / VERIFY

Pin the actual publisher

Readers independently establish which wallet to trust. A service name or shared recovery link alone isn't proof of ownership.

03 / REDISCOVER

Find the authorized route

The reader queries Arkiv directly, checks current record authority and presents available evidence without inventing live-uptime guarantees.

TRUST IS EXPLICIT

Recovery you can inspect.
Not just take on faith.

RelayLine uses public, wallet-attributed Arkiv records and an independent reader. Monitor observations may be missing or disputed, backups can fail, and this testnet prototype is not a security-audited availability guarantee.
